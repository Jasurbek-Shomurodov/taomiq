# 02 · Market research

Researched 23 Nov 2026 from live sites, Telegram channels and press. Each section states how reliable its numbers are. Sources are listed at the end.

## Key findings

1. **`taomiq.uz` is unregistered** (cctld.uz whois: not found). No brand named "Taomiq" was found on the web. Instagram/Telegram handles and the trademark register were not checked.
2. **"Mazali" is not a ration competitor.** `mazali.uz` redirects to `mazaly.uz`, a restaurant booking platform; "Mazzali" is a chain of ready-food shops. The real benchmark in Tashkent is **Food Star**.
3. **No Tashkent player has real online checkout.** Every one ends in a form, then a manager's call or Telegram chat, with Payme/Click paid by details the manager sends. A site where you configure, pay and manage a plan yourself would be the first in the market.
4. **Premium is nearly empty.** Above ~260,000 UZS/day there are two players: Green Point (detox-led, dormant Telegram) and Health Concept "Premium" (sold through Telegram). Neither has a strong site or a fitness/high-protein focus.
5. **The Russian market leader collapsed in 2026.** Level Kitchen (34% share in 2024) is in bankruptcy; the segment fell from 17bn RUB (2024) to about 3bn in H1 2026 as buyers moved to cheaper single ready meals. In the US, Fresh n' Lean (2024) and Freshly (2023) closed. The model is retention- and logistics-sensitive — the plan must be built on unit economics, not on growth by discount.
6. **The best current models are in the Gulf** (Calo, Delicut, Right Bite): daily fresh delivery, per-person macros, self-service pause/skip/swap, dietitian access, transparent tiers.

### What their sites do

**Homepage pattern (all leaders):** promo bar → hero with one promise, one button and "from X per day" → 3-step how it works → **program selector with sample menu by day, kcal and macros** → duration/price calculator → transformation stories → chef, nutritionist, production, certificates → delivery map with address check → FAQ → repeat call to action.

**Checkout pattern:** goal → calories → duration → days format (weekdays / full week) → live total → phone and promo code → address and slot → payment. Everyone captures the phone number before payment; cash to courier on first delivery is still offered.

**Table stakes:** calorie ladder; sample menu with macros; live price calculator; dish swaps and exclusions; pause with a stated cutoff; free delivery in a 2-hour slot; first-order promo; referral; account or app; address check.

**Differentiators of the best:**
- Good/better/best tiers with service differences (Justfood).
- Quantified customer stories, each with its own call to action (Grow Food).
- Favourites and stop-list that learn; courier preferences ("don't call, leave at door").
- Weekly billing or instalments for long plans.
- Family/second-ration discount.
- Packaging take-back.
- An honest nutrition stance as a brand asset (Justfood refuses keto and detox).

**Pricing architecture:**
- Duration discounts are shallow: about 5% for 2 weeks, 8–13% for a month, 15–20% for 2–4 months.
- Premium costs about 2.3× mass at the same calories.
- Price rises roughly linearly with calories.
- Paid add-ons: narrower slot, daily delivery, outer zone.

### What customers hate (review sites, app stores)

1. Late or missed deliveries without warning.
2. Refunds for unused days that never arrive; unreachable support.
3. Food near its expiry — a result of delivering every 2–3 days.
4. Final price higher than the advertised "from" price.
5. Paid swaps and surprise fees.
6. Quality drift: bland, repetitive, low protein, photos better than reality.
7. Small portions on low-calorie lines.
8. Aggressive sales calls after leaving a phone number.
9. Bonus programmes that don't credit.
10. No in-app cancel.

Each of these is a design requirement for TAOMIQ — see [03-product-strategy.md](03-product-strategy.md).

### Failures and their causes

| Company | What happened | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Level Kitchen (RU) | Bankruptcy 2026 after a 34% revenue drop | A consumer downturn sends customers to cheaper single meals; keep costs flexible |
| Fresh n' Lean (US) | Closed overnight in 2024, "by lenders" | Do not fund national cold-chain shipping with debt |
| Freshly (US) | Closed 2023 after a $950m acquisition | Retention fell; a single sales channel was too narrow |
| Calo UK | Exit after ~14 months | Do not expand to a second city before the first is dense and profitable |
| Love Yourself (UK) | Administration | Dependence on deep deal-site discounts |

### Trends 2025–2026

- Protein is the headline number (30–65 g per meal).
- Per-person macro targets, "enter your own macros".
- Dietitian access as part of the product.
- Less subscription lock-in: easy pause and cancel.
- App-first service with wearable data.
- "Clean label" claims (no seed oils, no added sugar).
- GLP-1-support plans (small portions, high protein) — standard in the US and Gulf; not relevant for Tashkent's launch.
- Offline and partner channels (gyms, retail corners, corporate).

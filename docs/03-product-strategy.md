# 03 · Product strategy

What TAOMIQ sells, to whom, at what price, and with which service promises. Everything here is a **proposal** derived from [02-market-research.md](02-market-research.md) and the brandbook; items needing the founders' decision are collected in [08-open-questions.md](08-open-questions.md).

## 1. Positioning

> **TAOMIQ — premium daily nutrition for Tashkent. Cooked fresh every day, counted to the gram, ordered and managed online in a minute.**

Three things no local competitor combines:

| Pillar | What it means | Who fails at it today |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh daily** | Cooked overnight, delivered every morning, seven days a week | Russian leaders deliver every 2–3 days; Healthy Food skips weekends |
| **Transparent** | Real upcoming menu, weights, КБЖУ, composition and allergens visible before payment; one honest price | Most hide prices or menus; "from" prices change at checkout |
| **Self-service** | Pay with Payme/Click, pause, skip, change address or slot in the account or Telegram — no manager needed | Every Tashkent player routes through a manager |

## 3. Launch lineup

The brandbook recommends launching with three programs. Proposed set, covering the three stated use cases (diet, fitness, high protein):

| Program | Calorie levels | Meals/day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LIGHT** | 1200 · 1500 | 4–5 | Calorie control. No level below 1200 — consistent with "no miracle promises" |
| **BALANCE** | 1800 · 2200 | 5 | Everyday balance |
| **PROTEIN** | 2000 · 2500 | 5–6 | High protein; daily protein grams shown as the headline number |

Phase 2: **GAIN** (3000+), **ACTIVE**, **OFFICE** (lunch-only, B2B), after demand is proven.

That is 6 SKUs at launch. Fewer SKUs mean a smaller kitchen matrix, less waste and consistent quality while volumes are low.

**Menu:** a **30-day cycle without repeats** per program (competitors offer 16–20 days). This is how "30 days, 30 different" is delivered. Every dish has a verified technological card before it is published.

**Standards stated on the site:** halal, no added sugar, no pork, cooked the night before delivery, 24-hour shelf life.

## 4. Pricing proposal

Market reference (Food Star, 21+ days): 155,000 at 1200 kcal · 165,000 at 1500 · 188,000 at 2000 · 225,000 at 2500. Premium players: 255,000–400,000.

Research shows premium at about 2.3× mass in mature markets, but Tashkent's premium tier is untested at scale. A **1.4–1.6× premium over Food Star** is a safer entry: clearly premium, still well below Green Point.

**Illustrative price grid, UZS per day (30-day price) — to be validated against food cost:**

| Program | Level | 30 days | 14 days (+5%) | 7 days (+10%) | Trial 2 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LIGHT | 1200 | 215,000 | 226,000 | 237,000 | 237,000 |
| LIGHT | 1500 | 235,000 | 247,000 | 259,000 | 259,000 |
| BALANCE | 1800 | 255,000 | 268,000 | 281,000 | 281,000 |
| BALANCE | 2200 | 285,000 | 299,000 | 314,000 | 314,000 |
| PROTEIN | 2000 | 295,000 | 310,000 | 325,000 | 325,000 |
| PROTEIN | 2500 | 340,000 | 357,000 | 374,000 | 374,000 |

Rules:
- **One price everywhere.** The price on the program card equals the price at checkout. Delivery inside the zone, dish swaps and exclusions are included.
- **Quiet duration ladder** instead of promotions: 7 days base, 14 days −5%, 30 days −10%. This matches the shallow ladders of healthy operators and the brandbook's ban on aggressive discounts.
- **Trial:** 2 days at the 7-day rate, no discount gimmick; the incentive is no commitment.
- **Weekdays-only option** (5 days a week) priced per delivered day.
- **Second ration to the same address:** −5%.
- **Referral:** friend gets 5% off the first order, referrer gets one bonus day.

**Unit economics to confirm before fixing prices** (per delivery day):

| Line | Target |
|---|---|
| Food cost | ≤ 35–40% of price |
| Packaging | 6,000 UZS (brandbook target) |
| Delivery | cost per drop on a dense morning route |
| Payment commission | 2–2.5% |
| Contribution margin | ≥ 25–35% |

## 5. Service promises (each answers a documented complaint)

| Promise | Complaint it answers |
|---|---|
| Delivery every morning in a chosen 2-hour window, 7 days a week; Telegram notice the evening before and when the courier is on the way | Late or missed deliveries without warning; narrow windows |
| Cooked the night before, 24-hour shelf life | Near-expiry food from 2–3-day delivery |
| Published refund rule: unused days are refunded to the original payment method within a stated number of days | Refunds that never arrive |
| Pause, skip and move a day in the account until a fixed evening cutoff (proposed 14:00 the day before) | Manager-dependent changes |
| Swaps and up to 3 ingredient exclusions included | Paid swaps and surprise fees |
| The price you see is the price you pay | "From" prices that grow at checkout |
| The full 30-day menu is public | Menus hidden until after payment |
| We call only if you ask us to | Aggressive sales calls |
| Dish ratings feed weekly menu corrections | Quality drift |

## 6. What we deliberately do not do at launch

- No native mobile app — a fast mobile site plus a Telegram bot covers the need; a Telegram Mini App is the phase-2 step.
- No free-form "build every meal" constructor — it multiplies kitchen complexity. Swaps and exclusions are enough.
- No medical or therapeutic programs (diabetic, keto, detox) — they require clinical backing and contradict the brand voice.
- No delivery outside Tashkent.
- No auto-renewing card subscription — prepaid packages with a renewal reminder first; saved-card renewal in phase 2.
- No bonus-points programme — referral only, until retention data exists.

## 7. Success measures for the first 6 months

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Trial → paid plan conversion | ≥ 40% |
| Share of orders completed without a manager | ≥ 70% |
| Monthly churn of 30-day customers | ≤ 12% |
| On-time deliveries | ≥ 97% |
| Average dish rating | ≥ 4.5 / 5 |
| Google + Yandex Maps reviews | 100+ at ≥ 4.7 |
| Contribution margin per delivery day | ≥ 25% |
